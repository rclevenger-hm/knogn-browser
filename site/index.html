Knogn is an experimental desktop browser built around a simple idea: your browser should work for you without turning what it knows into telemetry, profiling, or mandatory cloud state.

Download Knogn View source
Built differently

Privacy without theatre.

Knogn keeps browser-owned telemetry out, blocks third-party state by default, provides memory-only private profiles, and treats performance and compatibility as measured engineering requirements.

◇

Privacy by design

No Knogn analytics account, no mandatory cloud profile, and explicit controls around tracking surfaces and permissions.

↗

Fast by omission

Less background machinery, aggressive tab lifecycle management, and benchmarks that keep performance claims accountable.

◉

Open and inspectable

The browser, privacy contracts, performance guardrails, release pipelines, and current limitations live in the open.
